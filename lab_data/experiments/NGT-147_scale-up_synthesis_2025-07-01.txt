OBJECTIVE:
Scale up NGT-147 synthesis from 100mg to 10g for IND-enabling studies.
Previous small-scale synthesis gave 23% yield, need to maintain or improve.

PROCEDURE:

Step 1: 4-Chloro-6-methoxyquinazoline (50g, 1.0 eq) was dissolved in DMF (500mL) 
and heated to 80°C. 3-Fluoro-4-aminophenol (55g, 1.1 eq) was added slowly over 
30 minutes. Reaction stirred for 4 hours at 80°C.

TLC: Rf = 0.3 (50% EtOAc/hexanes)
LCMS: [M+H]+ = 289.1 (expected 289.1)

Step 2: Intermediate A (45g) was dissolved in toluene (400mL) and degassed with 
N2 for 30 minutes. 4-(2-Methoxyethoxy)phenylboronic acid (60g, 1.2 eq) and 
Pd(PPh3)4 (2.5g, 0.05 eq) were added. Reaction heated to 100°C for 6 hours.

TLC: Rf = 0.4 (50% EtOAc/hexanes)
LCMS: [M+H]+ = 375.2 (expected 375.2)

Step 3: Intermediate B (40g) was dissolved in DCM (300mL) and cooled to 0°C. 
BBr3 (120mL, 1.2M in DCM) was added dropwise over 1 hour. Reaction stirred 
at room temperature for 2 hours.

TLC: Rf = 0.2 (50% EtOAc/hexanes)
LCMS: [M+H]+ = 361.1 (expected 361.1)

Step 4: Intermediate C (35g) was dissolved in DMF (350mL) and heated to 100°C. 
2-Fluoro-4-aminophenol (38g, 1.1 eq) was added and reaction stirred for 6 hours.

TLC: Rf = 0.5 (50% EtOAc/hexanes)
LCMS: [M+H]+ = 456.5 (expected 456.5)

WORKUP:
Reaction mixture cooled to room temperature and poured into water (1L). 
Extracted with EtOAc (3 x 500mL). Combined organic layers washed with 
brine (500mL), dried over Na2SO4, and concentrated.

PURIFICATION:
Crude product purified by flash chromatography (SiO2, 0-50% EtOAc/hexanes).
Pure fractions combined and concentrated to give NGT-147 as white solid.

RESULTS:
- Yield: 8.1g (23% overall)
- Purity: 98.5% by HPLC
- Appearance: White crystalline solid
- Melting Point: 156-158°C
