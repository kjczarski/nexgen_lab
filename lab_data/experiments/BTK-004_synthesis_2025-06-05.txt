## Objective
Synthesize BTK-004 with 8-carbon linker to improve degradation potency and duration.

## Procedure

### Step 1: BTK Ligand Preparation
BTK ligand (2.5g, 1.0 eq) was dissolved in DMF (50mL) and cooled to 0°C. 
TEA (3.5mL, 2.5 eq) was added followed by Boc-protected linker (3.2g, 1.2 eq).
Reaction stirred at room temperature for 4 hours.

TLC: Rf = 0.6 (30% EtOAc/hexanes)
LCMS: [M+H]+ = 445.2 (expected 445.2)

### Step 2: Linker Deprotection
Intermediate A (2.8g) was dissolved in DCM (30mL) and TFA (15mL) was added.
Reaction stirred at room temperature for 2 hours.

TLC: Rf = 0.4 (30% EtOAc/hexanes)
LCMS: [M+H]+ = 345.1 (expected 345.1)

### Step 3: PROTAC Assembly
Intermediate B (2.5g) was dissolved in DMF (40mL) and cooled to 0°C.
CRBN ligand (2.1g, 1.1 eq) and HATU (1.8g, 1.2 eq) were added.
Reaction stirred at room temperature for 6 hours.

TLC: Rf = 0.3 (50% EtOAc/hexanes)
LCMS: [M+H]+ = 678.4 (expected 678.4)

## Workup and Purification
Reaction mixture poured into water (200mL) and extracted with EtOAc (3 x 100mL).
Combined organic layers washed with brine (100mL), dried over Na2SO4, and concentrated.
Crude product purified by flash chromatography (SiO2, 0-60% EtOAc/hexanes).

## Results
- **Yield:** 1.2g (18% overall)
- **Purity:** 96.2% by HPLC
- **Appearance:** White crystalline solid
- **Melting Point:** 142-144°C
