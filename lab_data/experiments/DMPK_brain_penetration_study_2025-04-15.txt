DMPK BRAIN PENETRATION STUDY - NGT-147
=======================================

Study ID: DMPK-2025-0415
Compound: NGT-147
Study Type: Brain Penetration Assessment
Date: April 15, 2025
Lead Scientist: Dr. Jonathan Hughes
Technician: Elena Popov

OBJECTIVE:
Evaluate brain penetration of NGT-147 in male Sprague-Dawley rats following 
intravenous administration. This is CRITICAL for the Go/No-Go decision.

METHODS:
Animals: 12 male Sprague-Dawley rats (200-250g)
Dose: 5 mg/kg IV (formulated in 20% HP-β-CD)
Timepoints: 0.25, 0.5, 1, 2, 4, 8 hours (n=2 per timepoint)
Sample collection: Blood and brain tissue
Bioanalysis: LC-MS/MS method (validated)

RESULTS:
Time (h) | Plasma Conc (ng/mL) | Brain Conc (ng/g) | Brain:Plasma Ratio
---------|-------------------|-------------------|-------------------
0.25     | 1250              | 2100              | 1.68
0.5      | 980               | 1650              | 1.68
1.0      | 720               | 1296              | 1.80
2.0      | 450               | 810               | 1.80
4.0      | 280               | 504               | 1.80
8.0      | 95                | 171               | 1.80

KEY FINDINGS:
- Brain:plasma ratio = 1.80 (EXCELLENT - target was >1.0)
- Consistent ratio across all timepoints
- Good brain exposure maintained for 8 hours
- No signs of toxicity or behavioral changes

COMPARISON TO PRECLINICAL CANDIDATES:
- NGT-112: 0.3 (rejected due to poor brain penetration)
- NGT-140: 0.4 (insufficient for brain tumors)
- NGT-145: 1.2 (acceptable but not optimal)

CONCLUSION:
NGT-147 shows EXCEPTIONAL brain penetration with brain:plasma ratio of 1.8.
This exceeds our target of >1.0 and supports advancement to IND-enabling studies.
The consistent ratio across timepoints indicates good brain distribution.

RECOMMENDATION: PROCEED to Go/No-Go meeting with strong recommendation for advancement.

Next steps:

- Include in IND package
- Consider higher doses for efficacy studies
