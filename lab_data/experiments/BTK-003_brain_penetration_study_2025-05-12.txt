## Objective
Test BTK-003 in brain penetration assay based on unusual PK profile observed.

## Background
Jonathan Hughes suggested testing BTK-003 for brain penetration after 
noticing unusual secondary peak in PK curve. LogP 2.8 is in CNS sweet spot.

## Procedure

### Brain Penetration Assay
1. **Dosing:** BTK-003 administered at 5 mg/kg IV to male Sprague-Dawley rats
2. **Time Points:** 0.5, 1, 2, 4, 8, 24 hours
3. **Tissues:** Plasma, brain, liver, kidney
4. **Analysis:** LC-MS/MS quantification

### Results
- **Brain:Plasma Ratio:** 1.2 at 4 hours (unexpected!)
- **Brain Cmax:** 0.8 μg/g at 4 hours
- **Plasma Cmax:** 0.7 μg/mL at 1 hour
- **Secondary Peak:** Confirmed at 4 hours in plasma

## Analysis
The brain penetration is surprisingly good for a PROTAC. Most PROTACs 
show ratios <0.3, but BTK-003 achieved 1.2. This suggests the compound 
is actually crossing the BBB effectively.

The secondary peak in plasma at 4 hours might be due to redistribution 
from brain tissue back to plasma. This is a known phenomenon for 
brain-penetrant compounds.

## Discussion
This is exciting! BTK-003 might be our first brain-penetrant PROTAC. 
The molecular properties (LogP 2.8, PSA 85 Å²) seem to be in the 
right range for CNS penetration.

**Next Steps:**
- Test BTK-003 in brain tumor models
- Optimize linker for better brain penetration
- Consider CNS indications
