# FDA Pre-IND Meeting Request

**Subject:** Pre-IND Meeting Request for NGT-147 (EGFR Kinase Inhibitor)

## Meeting Request Details

**Compound:** NGT-147  
**Indication:** Treatment of EGFR-mutant non-small cell lung cancer with brain metastases  
**Meeting Type:** Pre-IND Meeting  
**Requested Date:** August 15, 2025  
**Meeting Format:** Teleconference (preferred) or in-person  
**Duration:** 2 hours

## Meeting Objectives

1. **Discuss IND-enabling study design** for NGT-147
2. **Review safety assessment strategy** including cardiac safety
3. **Clarify regulatory requirements** for brain penetration claims
4. **Discuss clinical trial design** for Phase I studies
5. **Address any concerns** regarding the development program

## Background Information

### Compound Profile
- **Mechanism:** Irreversible EGFR kinase inhibitor
- **Target:** EGFR L858R, Del19, and T790M mutations
- **Key Differentiator:** Enhanced brain penetration (brain:plasma ratio = 1.8)
- **Development Stage:** Preclinical candidate selection complete

### Preclinical Data Summary
- **EGFR IC50:** 0.8 nM (excellent potency)
- **Selectivity:** >1000x vs other kinases
- **Brain Penetration:** 1.8-fold higher in brain vs plasma
- **Safety:** Clean hERG profile (IC50 >30 μM)
- **Efficacy:** 78% tumor growth inhibition in brain metastasis PDX model

### Proposed Development Plan
- **Phase I:** Dose escalation in EGFR-mutant NSCLC patients
- **Phase II:** Expansion in brain metastasis patients
- **Phase III:** Pivotal study vs standard of care

## Specific Questions for FDA

### 1. Safety Assessment
- **Cardiac Safety:** Are additional cardiac safety studies required beyond hERG?
- **CNS Safety:** What CNS safety assessments are needed for brain-penetrant compounds?
- **Reproductive Toxicity:** Can we defer reproductive tox studies to Phase II?

### 2. Clinical Trial Design
- **Starting Dose:** Recommended starting dose for Phase I?
- **Brain Metastasis:** Can we include brain metastasis patients in Phase I?
- **Biomarkers:** Required biomarker assessments for EGFR mutations?

### 3. Regulatory Strategy
- **Fast Track:** Eligibility for Fast Track designation?
- **Breakthrough Therapy:** Potential for Breakthrough Therapy designation?
- **Orphan Drug:** Eligibility for orphan drug designation?

### 4. Manufacturing
- **CMC Requirements:** Specific CMC requirements for IND submission?
- **Stability:** Required stability data for clinical supplies?
