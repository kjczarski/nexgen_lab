BACKGROUND OF THE INVENTION:

The present invention relates to novel pyrimidine-based compounds that inhibit EGFR kinase activity with enhanced brain penetration properties. These compounds are useful for treating cancers, particularly brain metastases and CNS-involved malignancies.

EGFR (Epidermal Growth Factor Receptor) is a well-established target in cancer therapy. However, existing EGFR inhibitors such as erlotinib, gefitinib, and osimertinib have limited brain penetration, restricting their utility in treating brain metastases.

The present invention addresses this limitation by providing compounds with improved brain penetration while maintaining potent EGFR inhibition and selectivity.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:

Example 1: Synthesis of NGT-147

Step 1: 4-Chloro-6-methoxyquinazoline (1.0 eq) was reacted with 3-fluoro-4-aminophenol (1.1 eq) in DMF at 80°C for 4 hours to give intermediate A.

Step 2: Intermediate A was subjected to Suzuki coupling with 4-(2-methoxyethoxy)phenylboronic acid (1.2 eq) using Pd(PPh3)4 catalyst to give intermediate B.

Step 3: Intermediate B was deprotected using BBr3 in DCM to give intermediate C.

Step 4: Intermediate C was coupled with 2-fluoro-4-aminophenol (1.1 eq) in DMF at 100°C for 6 hours to give NGT-147.

Yield: 23% overall
Purity: 98.5% by HPLC

Example 2: Biological Activity

NGT-147 was tested for EGFR kinase inhibition using standard TR-FRET assay:
- EGFR IC50: 0.8 nM
- CDK2 IC50: >10,000 nM
- CDK4 IC50: >10,000 nM
- CDK6 IC50: >10,000 nM

Example 3: Brain Penetration Study

NGT-147 was administered to male Sprague-Dawley rats at 5 mg/kg IV:
- Brain:plasma ratio: 1.8
- Brain Cmax: 2.1 μg/g
- Plasma Cmax: 1.2 μg/mL
