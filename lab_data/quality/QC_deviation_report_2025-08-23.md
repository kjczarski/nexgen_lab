# Quality Control Deviation Report

**Report ID:** QC-DEV-2025-0823  
**Date:** August 23, 2025  
**Reported by:** Dr. Hannah Lee  
**Reviewed by:** Dr. Carlos Mendez  
**Approved by:** Dr. Michael Torres

## Deviation Summary

**Type:** Assay Performance Deviation  
**Severity:** Minor  
**Impact:** Low  
**Status:** Resolved

## Description

During routine CDK2 screening assay on August 23, 2025, the Z' factor for the positive control (staurosporine) was measured at 0.42, which is below the acceptable threshold of 0.5.

## Details

**Assay:** CDK2 TR-FRET Kinase Assay  
**Plate:** 384-well white plate  
**Control:** Staurosporine (positive control)  
**Expected Z' Factor:** >0.5  
**Observed Z' Factor:** 0.42  
**Assay Date:** August 23, 2025  
**Technician:** Amy Chen

## Root Cause Analysis

**Investigation conducted by:** Dr. Hannah Lee  
**Date:** August 23, 2025

### Possible Causes Investigated:
1. **Substrate Quality:** Checked substrate lot - within specification
2. **Enzyme Activity:** CDK2 enzyme activity normal
3. **Plate Quality:** No visible defects in 384-well plate
4. **Temperature:** Incubator temperature stable at 37°C
5. **Timing:** Incubation time consistent with protocol

### Root Cause Identified:
**Substrate Concentration:** The TR-FRET detection reagent was prepared at 90% of the specified concentration due to a pipetting error during reagent preparation.

## Corrective Actions

### Immediate Actions (August 23, 2025):
1. **Reagent Preparation:** Re-prepared TR-FRET detection reagent at correct concentration
2. **Assay Repeat:** Re-ran the CDK2 screening assay with corrected reagent
3. **Quality Check:** Confirmed Z' factor >0.5 for repeated assay

### Preventive Actions:
1. **Training:** Provided additional training to Amy Chen on reagent preparation
2. **Protocol Update:** Added double-check step for reagent concentration
3. **Documentation:** Updated SOP to include concentration verification step

## Impact Assessment

### Data Impact:
- **Affected Assays:** 1 CDK2 screening plate (384 compounds)
- **Data Status:** Invalidated due to poor Z' factor
- **Re-test Required:** Yes - completed August 23, 2025

### Timeline Impact:
- **Delay:** 4 hours (time to re-prepare reagent and re-run assay)
- **Project Impact:** Minimal - no impact on CDK2 program timeline

### Cost Impact:
- **Additional Reagents:** $150
- **Labor Cost:** 4 hours × $75/hour = $300
- **Total Cost:** $450

## Verification

**Verification Date:** August 23, 2025  
**Verified by:** Dr. Carlos Mendez

### Results:
- **Z' Factor:** 0.78 (excellent)
- **Assay Performance:** Within specification
- **Data Quality:** Acceptable for analysis

## Lessons Learned

1. **Double-checking:** Critical importance of verifying reagent concentrations
2. **Training:** Need for regular refresher training on assay protocols
3. **Documentation:** Importance of clear SOPs with verification steps

## Follow-up Actions

### Short-term (Next 30 days):
1. **Training Review:** Conduct training review for all screening technicians
2. **Protocol Audit:** Review all screening protocols for similar issues
3. **Quality Monitoring:** Increase frequency of Z' factor monitoring

### Long-term (Next 90 days):
1. **SOP Updates:** Update all screening SOPs with verification steps
2. **Training Program:** Develop comprehensive training program for new technicians
3. **Quality Metrics:** Establish regular quality metrics review
