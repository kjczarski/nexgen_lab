## Executive Summary

The Q3 2025 internal quality audit of the Discovery Division was conducted to assess compliance with Good Laboratory Practice (GLP) standards and internal quality procedures. The audit covered laboratory operations, data integrity, equipment maintenance, and personnel training.

**Overall Assessment:** Compliant with minor findings  
**Risk Level:** Low  
**Recommendations:** 3 minor findings requiring corrective action

## Audit Scope

### Areas Audited
- **Laboratory Operations:** Standard operating procedures, work practices
- **Data Integrity:** Lab notebooks, electronic records, data management
- **Equipment Maintenance:** Calibration, maintenance records, usage logs
- **Personnel Training:** Training records, competency assessments
- **Quality Systems:** Document control, change management, CAPA

## Findings

### Major Findings
**None identified**

### Minor Findings

#### Finding 1: Lab Notebook Documentation
- **Description:** Inconsistent lab notebook documentation across teams
- **Risk:** Medium
- **Evidence:** 15% of lab notebooks missing required signatures
- **Root Cause:** Inadequate training on documentation requirements
- **Corrective Action:** Update training program and conduct refresher training

#### Finding 2: Equipment Calibration
- **Description:** UPLC system calibration overdue by 2 weeks
- **Risk:** Low
- **Evidence:** Calibration certificate expired August 30, 2025
- **Root Cause:** Scheduling oversight
- **Corrective Action:** Immediate calibration and improved scheduling system

#### Finding 3: Data Backup
- **Description:** Incomplete data backup procedures for electronic records
- **Risk:** Medium
- **Evidence:** 20% of electronic data not backed up according to schedule
- **Root Cause:** Inadequate backup monitoring system
- **Corrective Action:** Implement automated backup monitoring

### Observations

#### Positive Observations
1. **Safety Culture:** Excellent safety awareness and practices
2. **Data Quality:** High-quality scientific data and documentation
3. **Team Collaboration:** Strong cross-functional collaboration
4. **Innovation:** Significant scientific achievements (NGT-147, BTK-003)

#### Areas for Improvement
1. **Documentation:** Standardize documentation practices

4. **Data Management:** Strengthen data backup procedures
