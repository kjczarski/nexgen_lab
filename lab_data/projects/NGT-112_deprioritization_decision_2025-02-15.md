# NGT-112 Deprioritization Decision - February 15, 2025

**Background:**
NGT-112 was our lead EGFR inhibitor candidate until February 2025. The compound showed excellent potency (EGFR IC50 = 0.6 nM) and good selectivity, but was deprioritized due to safety concerns identified during DMPK profiling.

## Key Findings Leading to Deprioritization

### DMPK Results (Dr. Jonathan Hughes)
- **hERG IC50: 2.3 μM** - This is a major red flag
- FDA guidelines require hERG IC50 >30 μM for safe development
- Risk of QT prolongation and cardiac arrhythmias
- No clear path to improve hERG profile without losing potency

### Additional Concerns
- **Brain penetration: 0.3** - Insufficient for brain metastasis indication
- **Metabolic stability: 35%** - Poor, would require frequent dosing
- **CYP inhibition: 3A4 IC50 = 8 μM** - Drug-drug interaction risk

## Decision Process

**February 10, 2025:** Jonathan Hughes identified hERG liability
**February 12, 2025:** Emergency meeting with Sarah Chen, Rachel Foster, Patricia O'Brien
**February 15, 2025:** Final decision to deprioritize NGT-112

**Rationale:**
1. **Safety Risk:** hERG liability is unacceptable for clinical development
2. **Regulatory Risk:** FDA would likely require extensive cardiac safety studies
3. **Competitive Risk:** Better compounds (NGT-147) available in pipeline
4. **Resource Efficiency:** Focus resources on NGT-147 instead

## Lessons Learned

1. **Early DMPK Screening:** hERG assessment should be done earlier in discovery
2. **Safety-First Approach:** Better to deprioritize early than fail late
3. **Backup Compounds:** Always maintain multiple candidates
4. **Cross-functional Review:** DMPK input critical for go/no-go decisions

## Impact on Program

**Positive Outcomes:**
- Resources redirected to NGT-147 (better overall profile)
- Improved DMPK screening cascade implemented
- Earlier safety assessment protocols established

**Resource Savings:**
- Avoided $2M+ in failed clinical development costs
- Saved 18 months of development time
- Prevented potential regulatory issues

## Current Status

NGT-112 is archived but not destroyed. Compound samples maintained for:
- Future reference
- Patent applications
- Structure-activity relationship analysis

**Replacement:** NGT-147 advanced as lead candidate (March 2025)
