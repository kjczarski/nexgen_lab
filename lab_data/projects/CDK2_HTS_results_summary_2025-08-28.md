# CDK2 High-Throughput Screening Results Summary

**Study:** CDK2 Selective Inhibitor Discovery  
**Date:** August 28, 2025  
**Lead Scientist:** Dr. Carlos Mendez  
**Screening Team:** Hannah Lee, Robert Taylor, Amy Chen, Michael Brown

## Executive Summary

Completed high-throughput screening campaign against CDK2 kinase using our proprietary compound library. Screened 125,000 compounds at 10 μM concentration. Identified 1,247 primary hits (>50% inhibition), reduced to 23 validated hits after secondary screening and triage.

## Screening Protocol

**Primary Screen:**
- Library: NexGen proprietary kinase-focused library
- Concentration: 10 μM
- Assay: TR-FRET CDK2 kinase assay
- Controls: Staurosporine (positive), DMSO (negative)
- Z' factor: 0.78 (excellent)

**Secondary Screen:**
- IC50 determination for all primary hits
- Selectivity vs CDK1, CDK4, CDK6
- Solubility assessment
- Chemical tractability evaluation

## Key Results

**Hit Statistics:**
- Primary hits: 1,247 (1.0% hit rate)
- Validated hits: 23 (1.8% validation rate)
- IC50 range: 0.5 - 15 μM
- Best IC50: 0.5 μM (CDK2-001)

**Selectivity Profile:**
- CDK2 vs CDK1: >100x selective (most compounds)
- CDK2 vs CDK4: >50x selective (18/23 compounds)
- CDK2 vs CDK6: >30x selective (21/23 compounds)

**Chemical Classes Identified:**
1. Pyrimidine-based inhibitors (8 compounds)
2. Pyridine-based inhibitors (6 compounds)
3. Indole-based inhibitors (4 compounds)
4. Quinazoline-based inhibitors (3 compounds)
5. Other scaffolds (2 compounds)
