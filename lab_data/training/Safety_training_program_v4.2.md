## Program Overview

The Laboratory Safety Training Program ensures all personnel are properly trained in laboratory safety procedures, chemical handling, and emergency response. This program is mandatory for all laboratory personnel and must be completed annually.

## Training Modules

### Module 1: General Laboratory Safety
- **Duration:** 2 hours
- **Format:** Classroom + Hands-on
- **Instructor:** Monica Reyes
- **Frequency:** Annual

#### Topics Covered
1. **Laboratory Rules and Regulations**
   - Personal protective equipment (PPE)
   - Laboratory access and security
   - Emergency procedures
   - Incident reporting

2. **Chemical Safety**
   - Chemical storage and handling
   - Safety data sheets (SDS)
   - Chemical compatibility
   - Waste disposal

3. **Equipment Safety**
   - Equipment operation and maintenance
   - Lockout/tagout procedures
   - Electrical safety
   - Compressed gas safety

### Module 2: Chemical Hygiene
- **Duration:** 3 hours
- **Format:** Classroom + Laboratory
- **Instructor:** Monica Reyes
- **Frequency:** Annual

#### Topics Covered
1. **Chemical Properties**
   - Physical and chemical properties
   - Hazard identification
   - Risk assessment
   - Exposure limits

2. **Safe Handling Practices**
   - Proper techniques for chemical handling
   - Spill response procedures
   - Emergency first aid
   - Decontamination procedures

3. **Personal Protection**
   - PPE selection and use
   - Respiratory protection
   - Eye and face protection
   - Hand protection

### Module 3: Emergency Response
- **Duration:** 2 hours
- **Format:** Classroom + Drills
- **Instructor:** Monica Reyes
- **Frequency:** Annual

#### Topics Covered
1. **Emergency Procedures**
   - Fire safety and evacuation
   - Chemical spill response
   - Medical emergencies
   - Emergency contacts

2. **Emergency Equipment**
   - Fire extinguishers
   - Emergency showers and eyewashes
   - First aid kits
   - Emergency communication systems

3. **Drill Procedures**
   - Fire drill procedures
   - Evacuation routes
   - Assembly points
   - Emergency communication

### Module 4: Specialized Training
- **Duration:** Variable (1-4 hours)
- **Format:** Laboratory-specific
- **Instructor:** Subject matter experts
- **Frequency:** As needed

#### Topics Covered
1. **Radiation Safety**
   - Radioactive material handling
   - Radiation monitoring
   - Contamination control
   - Waste disposal

2. **Biological Safety**
   - Biological agent handling
   - Containment procedures
   - Decontamination
   - Waste disposal

3. **Cryogenic Safety**
   - Liquid nitrogen handling
   - Cryogenic storage
   - Personal protection
   - Emergency procedures

## Training Requirements

### Mandatory Training
- **All Laboratory Personnel:** Modules 1-3
- **Chemical Handlers:** Modules 1-3 + Chemical Hygiene
- **Equipment Operators:** Modules 1-3 + Equipment Safety

### Training Frequency
