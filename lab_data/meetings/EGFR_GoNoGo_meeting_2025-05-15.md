# EGFR Go/No-Go Decision Meeting - May 15, 2025

**Date:** May 15, 2025  
**Time:** 2:00 PM - 4:30 PM

**Attendees:** Dr. Rebecca Chao (VP R&D), Dr. Michael Torres (Discovery Dir), Dr. Patricia O'Brien (Preclinical Dir), Dr. Sarah Chen (Med Chem), Dr. Rachel Foster (Biology), Dr. Linda Wu (Comp Chem), Dr. Jonathan Hughes (DMPK)

## Agenda Items

### 1. NGT-147 Program Status Review
**Presenter:** Dr. Sarah Chen

**Key Data Points:**
- Synthesized 15 analogs, NGT-147 shows best overall profile
- EGFR IC50: 0.8 nM (excellent potency)
- Selectivity vs other kinases: >1000x (clean profile)
- Solubility: 45 μM in PBS (acceptable for formulation)

**Presenter:** Dr. Rachel Foster

**Biology Results:**
- Cell proliferation IC50: 12 nM (A549 EGFR mutant)
- PDX model (lung cancer): 78% tumor growth inhibition at 50 mg/kg
- **CRITICAL:** Brain metastasis model showed 65% inhibition - this is huge!
- No significant toxicity in 2-week study

**Presenter:** Dr. Jonathan Hughes

**DMPK Results:**
- **BREAKTHROUGH:** Brain:plasma ratio = 1.8 (target was >1.0)
- Plasma half-life: 4.2 hours (good for QD dosing)
- Microsomal stability: 85% remaining after 30 min (excellent)
- No significant CYP inhibition
- hERG IC50: >30 μM (clean)

**Presenter:** Dr. Linda Wu

**Computational Validation:**
- Crystal structure confirms predicted binding mode
- FEP calculations within 0.5 kcal/mol of experimental data
- ADMET predictions validated across all parameters

## Decision

**RECOMMENDATION:** **GO** - Advance NGT-147 to IND-enabling studies

**Rationale:**
1. Excellent efficacy in brain metastasis models
2. Clean safety profile
3. Strong IP position
4. Clear differentiation vs competitors
5. Manageable development timeline
