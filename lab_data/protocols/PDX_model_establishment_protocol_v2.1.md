## Overview
This protocol describes the establishment and maintenance of patient-derived xenograft (PDX) models for oncology drug discovery at NexGen Therapeutics.

## Materials Required

### Animals
- **Strain:** NOD.Cg-Prkdcscid Il2rgtm1Wjl/SzJ (NSG) mice
- **Age:** 6-8 weeks
- **Weight:** 20-25g
- **Source:** Jackson Laboratory
- **Housing:** Specific pathogen-free (SPF) conditions

### Equipment
- Biosafety cabinet (Class II)
- CO2 incubator (37°C, 5% CO2)
- Stereotaxic apparatus
- Surgical instruments
- Anesthesia machine

### Reagents
- Matrigel (Corning, 354234)
- RPMI-1640 medium
- Fetal bovine serum (FBS)
- Penicillin-streptomycin
- Trypsin-EDTA
- Phosphate-buffered saline (PBS)

## Procedure

### 1. Tumor Tissue Processing
1. **Tissue Collection:** Obtain fresh tumor tissue from surgical specimens
2. **Processing:** Cut tissue into 2-3mm pieces
3. **Digestion:** Incubate with collagenase (1mg/mL) for 30 minutes
4. **Filtration:** Pass through 70μm cell strainer
5. **Centrifugation:** 300g for 5 minutes
6. **Resuspension:** In complete medium

### 2. Implantation
1. **Anesthesia:** Isoflurane (2-3% in oxygen)
2. **Site Preparation:** Subcutaneous flank injection
3. **Cell Suspension:** 1×10^6 cells in 100μL Matrigel
4. **Injection:** 27G needle, subcutaneous
5. **Monitoring:** Daily tumor measurements

### 3. Tumor Growth Monitoring
- **Measurements:** Caliper measurements 3x/week
- **Volume Calculation:** V = (L × W²) / 2
- **Passaging:** When tumor reaches 1000mm³
- **Cryopreservation:** Liquid nitrogen storage

## Quality Control

### Histology
- **H&E Staining:** Confirm tumor morphology
- **IHC:** EGFR, Ki67, cleaved caspase-3
- **Frequency:** Every 3 passages

### Molecular Characterization
- **EGFR Sequencing:** Confirm mutation status
- **Copy Number:** FISH analysis
- **Expression:** RNA-seq analysis

## Data Analysis

### Efficacy Assessment
- **Tumor Growth Inhibition:** %TGI = (1 - T/C) × 100
- **Statistical Analysis:** Two-way ANOVA
- **Significance:** p < 0.05

### Example Results (NGT-147)
- **Model:** NGT-PDX-005 (Brain Metastasis)
- **Dose:** 50 mg/kg QD
- **TGI:** 78% (p < 0.001)
- **Brain Penetration:** Confirmed by LC-MS/MS

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues
1. **Poor Engraftment:** Check cell viability, increase cell number
2. **Slow Growth:** Verify mouse strain, check housing conditions
3. **Contamination:** Maintain sterile technique, monitor health

### Success Rate
- **Overall:** 85% engraftment rate
- **Lung Cancer:** 90% success
- **Brain Metastasis:** 75% success
