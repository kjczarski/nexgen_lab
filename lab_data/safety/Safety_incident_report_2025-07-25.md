# Safety Incident Report

**Incident ID:** SAF-2025-0725  
**Date:** July 25, 2025  
**Time:** 2:30 PM  
**Location:** Building B, Floor 1, Lab Bay 2  
**Reported by:** Dr. Priya Sharma  
**Investigated by:** Monica Reyes (EH&S Specialist)  
**Approved by:** Dr. Michael Torres

## Incident Summary

**Type:** Chemical Spill  
**Severity:** Minor  
**Injuries:** None  
**Property Damage:** Minimal  
**Status:** Resolved

## Description

During routine synthesis of NGT-151, approximately 50 mL of ethyl acetate was spilled when a round-bottom flask was accidentally knocked over. The spill occurred in the fume hood and was contained within the hood surface.

## Immediate Response

### Actions Taken (within 5 minutes):
1. **Evacuation:** Lab personnel moved to safe distance
2. **Containment:** Spill contained within fume hood
3. **Notification:** EH&S specialist notified immediately
4. **Cleanup:** Spill cleaned using appropriate absorbent materials
5. **Ventilation:** Fume hood ventilation increased

### Personnel Involved:
- **Primary:** Dr. Priya Sharma (synthesis operator)
- **Witness:** Lisa Zhang (PhD student)
- **Response:** Monica Reyes (EH&S Specialist)

## Root Cause Analysis

### Investigation conducted by:** Monica Reyes  
**Date:** July 25, 2025

### Contributing Factors:
1. **Primary Cause:** Cluttered workspace in fume hood
2. **Secondary Cause:** Inadequate flask support
3. **Contributing Factor:** Rushed synthesis schedule

### Root Cause Identified:
**Workspace Organization:** The fume hood was cluttered with multiple reaction setups, reducing available space and increasing risk of accidental contact.

## Corrective Actions

### Immediate Actions (July 25, 2025):
1. **Cleanup:** Complete spill cleanup completed
2. **Workspace:** Fume hood decluttered and reorganized
3. **Training:** Refresher training provided to Priya Sharma
4. **Equipment:** Additional flask supports installed

### Preventive Actions:
1. **SOP Update:** Updated fume hood organization SOP
2. **Training:** Mandatory workspace safety training for all chemists
3. **Equipment:** Installed additional safety equipment
4. **Monitoring:** Increased safety inspections

## Impact Assessment

### Safety Impact:
- **Personnel:** No injuries or exposure
- **Environment:** Minimal environmental impact
- **Equipment:** No damage to equipment

### Operational Impact:
- **Synthesis Delay:** 2 hours (cleanup and reorganization)
- **Cost:** $150 (cleanup materials and equipment)
- **Schedule:** No impact on project timeline

### Regulatory Impact:
- **Reporting:** Incident reported to EH&S database
- **Documentation:** Complete incident documentation
- **Follow-up:** Safety inspection scheduled

## Lessons Learned

1. **Workspace Management:** Critical importance of organized workspace
2. **Safety Culture:** Need for continuous safety awareness
3. **Equipment:** Importance of proper equipment support
4. **Training:** Value of regular safety training

## Follow-up Actions

### Short-term (Next 30 days):
1. **Safety Inspection:** Complete safety inspection of all fume hoods
2. **Training Review:** Review safety training for all lab personnel
3. **Equipment Audit:** Audit all safety equipment

### Long-term (Next 90 days):
1. **SOP Updates:** Update all lab safety SOPs
2. **Training Program:** Develop comprehensive safety training program
3. **Safety Culture:** Implement safety culture improvement program
