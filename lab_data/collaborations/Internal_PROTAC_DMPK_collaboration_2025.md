# Research Collaboration Agreement - Internal

## Collaboration Overview

### Purpose
Joint research collaboration to develop CNS-penetrant PROTACs for neurological diseases, combining PROTAC expertise with brain penetration optimization.

### Scope
- **PROTAC Design:** Optimize PROTACs for CNS penetration
- **DMPK Modeling:** Predict brain penetration properties
- **Assay Development:** Develop CNS-specific assays
- **Target Validation:** Validate CNS targets for PROTACs

## Research Activities

### Phase 1 (Months 1-2): Proof of Concept
- **BTK-003 Analysis:** Complete brain penetration characterization
- **Model Validation:** Test brain penetration prediction models
- **Assay Development:** Optimize CNS penetration assays

### Phase 2 (Months 3-4): Optimization
- **Linker Design:** Optimize PROTAC linkers for CNS penetration
- **Property Optimization:** Balance degradation vs brain penetration
- **In Vivo Studies:** Test optimized PROTACs in brain models

### Phase 3 (Months 5-6): Validation
- **Target Expansion:** Apply to additional CNS targets
- **Mechanism Studies:** Investigate brain penetration mechanisms
- **Publication:** Prepare results for publication
