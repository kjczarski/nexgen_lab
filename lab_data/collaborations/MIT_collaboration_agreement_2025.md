# Collaboration Agreement - MIT

**Agreement Type:** Research Collaboration  
**Partner:** Massachusetts Institute of Technology

**Effective Date:** June 1, 2025  
**Duration:** 2 years

## Collaboration Overview

### Purpose
Joint research collaboration to develop novel PROTAC technology for challenging drug targets, with focus on E3 ligase discovery and optimization.

### Scope
- **E3 Ligase Discovery:** Identify new E3 ligases for PROTAC applications
- **PROTAC Optimization:** Improve PROTAC design and efficacy
- **Target Validation:** Validate novel PROTAC targets
- **Technology Transfer:** Share PROTAC technology and expertise

## Key Terms

### Intellectual Property
- **Joint IP:** Shared ownership of jointly developed IP
- **Background IP:** Each party retains ownership of background IP
- **Publication Rights:** Joint publication rights with 60-day review
- **Patent Rights:** Joint patent filing for collaborative inventions

### Financial Terms
- **NexGen Funding:** $500,000 over 2 years
- **MIT Contribution:** In-kind research support
- **Payment Schedule:** Quarterly payments of $62,500
- **Budget Review:** Annual budget review and adjustment
